# Name and Logo Policy

## The Quotapus Name and Logo

The Quotapus name, logo, app icon, and associated visual identity belong to livejiaquan. All rights reserved. The MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the source code, not the name or the logo, even where an icon file sits in this repository so the app can be built.

## What You Can Do

- Use "Quotapus" to refer to this project in articles, blog posts, reviews, and discussions
- Say your project is "compatible with Quotapus" or "built for Quotapus"
- Show unmodified screenshots of the app

## What You Cannot Do

- Use "Quotapus" as the name of a fork, derivative work, product, or service
- Use the Quotapus logo or app icon in your own project, product, or service, modified or not
- Imply that your project is endorsed by, affiliated with, or an official part of Quotapus

## Forks

If you fork Quotapus, you must:

1. Choose a different name for your fork
2. Replace the Quotapus logo, app icon, and branding with your own
3. Clearly state that your project is a fork and not the official Quotapus
